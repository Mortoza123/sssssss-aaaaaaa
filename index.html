Even when we disagree

Of course, no friendship is perfect every single day. Sometimes we misunderstand each other, get annoyed, or say things differently from how we mean them. But a difficult moment does not have to erase all the good moments around it. I hope we keep learning to listen, explain ourselves honestly, and give each other space when needed. Real friendship is not about never having problems; it is about treating each other with care through them.
