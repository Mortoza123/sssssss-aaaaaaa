HAPPY BIRTHDAY SIMA — Website

Birthday: 24 October
Relationship language: best friend, good friend, favourite person (no girlfriend wording)

HOW TO VIEW
1. Extract sima_birthday_website.zip.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Internet is needed for Google Fonts and placeholder images if you have not added your photos.

HOW TO ADD THE 15 PHOTOS
1. Open the photos folder.
2. Add your photos and name them photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, ... photo15.jpg.
3. If your photos are PNG, convert them to JPG or change the image extension in script.js.
4. Refresh index.html.

FILES
- index.html: birthday page and 10 personal paragraphs
- style.css: advanced romantic pink/rose design, large animated flower bouquets on both sides, swaying stems, glowing petals, and responsive animation styles
- script.js: animated starfield, falling flower petals, scroll reveals, photo gallery, mouse glow and sparkle button
- photos/: add your 15 images here

You can change the text directly in index.html.

MOBILE VIEW
The layout adapts to phone screens, including small screens around 320px wide. Open index.html on your phone or use your browser's device toolbar to preview mobile sizes. Reduced-motion settings are respected.

FIXED FLOWER PLACEMENT
The large animated flower bouquets are placed inside the hero section so they frame the birthday heading correctly on desktop and mobile.
